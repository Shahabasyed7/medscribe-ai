Clinical Guidance & Safety Notice

MedScribe AI utilizes real-time WebRTC Voice Activity Detection and Faster-Whisper ASR to transcribe doctor-patient consultations, followed by structured clinical summarization.

  • Clinician Verification: AI summaries are documentation aids only and must be thoroughly reviewed and verified by a licensed clinician before inclusion in medical records.
  • Zero Hallucination: Absent information defaults to null or empty collections rather than invented clinical facts.
  • Audio Security: Audio is processed in memory during the consultation and never written to permanent disk storage unless explicitly exported via "Save Recording".
